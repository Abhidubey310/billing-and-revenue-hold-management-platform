# Billing Automation & Revenue Assurance Transformation

**Portfolio case study | Finance Transformation | O2C | Billing Controls | Exception Management**

## Executive overview
Billing holds create avoidable manual work when reasons, ownership, ageing and potential billing exposure are fragmented across teams and systems. This independently recreated project demonstrates how a controlled workflow can make billing exceptions easier to investigate, prioritise and resolve.

## Business problem
Teams need to establish why an order is blocked, who owns the next action, how long it has been held and whether the value or risk requires escalation. Without a consistent framework, reporting and resolution can become reactive.

## Transformation approach
1. Map the current-state billing hold and exception workflow.
2. Define required data fields, hold categories and ownership rules.
3. Validate records before processing.
4. Apply deterministic classification, ageing and value-based prioritisation.
5. Generate an explainable action queue with recommended next steps.
6. Route ambiguous cases to manual review and retain approval controls.
7. Track backlog, exposure and improvement opportunities through management reporting.

## Demonstration and outcomes
The public demonstration uses **12 fictional held orders** and **£423.3k of illustrative billing value**. It categorises exceptions and generates an action queue. These figures are synthetic, not actual company exposure.

An accompanying ROI calculator illustrates how to estimate annual time released, support costs and payback. Its **1,104 hours and £18,288 net benefit** are assumption-based demonstration outputs, **not realised employer savings**.

## Professional experience connection
This project is informed by my work on billing transformation, exception analysis, process redesign, automation and revenue assurance. The published logic, sample data and outputs are independently recreated and do not disclose employer systems, customers, internal reports or proprietary controls.

## Control design
- Required-field and data-quality checks
- Explainable hold categories, owner routing and priority rules
- Manual-review route for unclassified cases
- Human authorisation before operational release or accounting action
- Traceable reason codes and reporting outputs

The prototype **does not automatically release billing holds or recognise revenue**. Enterprise integration is described as a potential architecture, not a claimed production deployment.
